Who We Are

A strategic funding partner for every stage of the grant journey

Agiele Funding is a U.S.-focused grant consultancy supporting organizations nationwide. Many firms stop at the application. We stay with you through the entire lifecycle: researching the right opportunities, shaping a funding strategy, preparing competitive applications, guiding you through approval, and managing administration and reporting until closeout.

The result is a single, accountable partner for your funding efforts, so your team can stay focused on the mission the funding is meant to advance.

How We Work

A six-stage process, managed for you

  1. 1

    Discover

    We start by understanding your objectives, programs, capacity, and what funding success looks like for you.

  2. 2

    Research

    We identify suitable federal, state, foundation, and corporate opportunities matched to your eligibility and goals.

  3. 3

    Apply

    We prepare and coordinate complete, competitive applications and manage every submission requirement.

  4. 4

    Award

    When good news arrives, we support you through approvals, agreements, and award requirements.

  5. 5

    Manage

    We assist with grant administration, documentation, and funder communications throughout the award period.

  6. 6

    Report

    We support progress reports, final reports, and closeout, protecting your reputation with every funder.
