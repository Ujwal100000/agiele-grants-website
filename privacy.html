Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Who we are

Agiele Funding ("we", "us") is a grant research, writing, and management consultancy serving organizations across the United States. This policy explains how we handle information collected through this website.

2. Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, organization, state, industry, funding needs, and message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and discuss our services with you.

3. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to your request, schedule consultations you ask for, and communicate about our services. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties. We do not use your information for automated decision-making.

4. Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and styling resources from a content delivery network, and scheduling links direct you to Calendly, whose own privacy policy applies when you use their booking service. Form submissions are transmitted securely to our data storage provider.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store standard technical data as part of normal operation.

6. Data retention and your choices

We retain inquiry records only as long as needed to respond and maintain a business relationship. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by emailing clientsuccess@agiele.com.

7. Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect submitted information. No internet transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the form.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: clientsuccess@agiele.com. Mail: Agiele Funding, 4257 Jackson St NE, Columbia Heights, MN 55421.
