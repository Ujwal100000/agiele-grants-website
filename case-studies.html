Vitlycare

Healthcare technology, Minnesota

vitlycare.com

Funding Program

Bold Path Fellowship

Award

$120,000

Outcome

Successful Award

Client introduction

Vitlycare builds rate management and intake software for Minnesota residential care providers, turning MnCHOICES packets and clinical documentation into defensible CRS/FRS rate ranges and DHS-ready workflows. The company's platform connects providers and case managers in one collaborative workspace.

The funding opportunity

Vitlycare identified the Bold Path Fellowship as a strong fit: a program designed to accelerate ambitious, mission-driven companies with funding and structured support at a pivotal growth stage.

Client objectives

As a growing health technology company, Vitlycare wanted to secure non-dilutive funding to accelerate product development and scale its platform for residential care providers across Minnesota.

How Agiele Funding supported the process

Agiele Funding worked with Vitlycare, with client representative Ola Oladeji, across the application journey: assessing eligibility and fit for the fellowship, shaping the application narrative around the company's vision and impact, coordinating the required materials, and supporting the process through to the award decision.

The application journey

The engagement followed our six-stage approach: discover the company's goals, research the program's selection criteria, prepare a compelling application, and support the client through the award stage, exactly as we do for every engagement.

The funding outcome

Vitlycare received a successful award of $120,000 through the Bold Path Fellowship, funding that supports the company's continued growth in residential care technology.

Client testimonial

“Agiele Funding took the guesswork out of a funding process we did not have the bandwidth to run ourselves. Their research was thorough, the application was sharp, and the outcome speaks for itself. We would not hesitate to work with them again.”

Ola Oladeji, Founder and CEO, Vitlycare
