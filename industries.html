Industries We Serve

Sector-aware funding strategy

Every sector funds differently. We tailor research, positioning, and applications to the programs, language, and reviewers of each industry we serve.

Featured

Healthcare and Medical Technology

Hospitals, clinics, provider organizations, and health technology companies pursue program funding, innovation grants, and workforce initiatives. We speak the language of outcomes, compliance, and patient impact.

Biotechnology and Life Sciences

Research-driven organizations pursuing translational science, early-stage validation, and commercialization funding, where rigorous scientific framing and milestones decide outcomes.

AI and Technology

Technology companies and AI developers pursuing innovation-focused programs and fellowships, with applications that translate technical ambition into funder-ready impact.

Startups and Entrepreneurship

Fellowships, pitch-linked funding, and non-dilutive programs for early-stage founders. We help you present a credible plan to reviewers who see hundreds of applications.

Small and Medium-Sized Businesses

Growth, export, workforce, and industry-specific programs for established businesses, matched to your revenue stage, headcount, and expansion plans.

Nonprofits

Program funding, general operating support, capacity building, and capital campaigns, with applications that connect community need to measurable outcomes.

Education and Research

Schools, districts, universities, and research groups pursuing education innovation, faculty support, and research funding with disciplined, evidence-based proposals.

Social Impact

Mission-driven organizations pursuing funding for equity, public health, workforce, and community wellbeing initiatives, with storytelling grounded in evidence.

Community Development

Housing, revitalization, and infrastructure-adjacent initiatives that depend on layered public and private funding sources, navigated with care.

Environmental Innovation

Clean energy, conservation, climate resilience, and sustainability projects pursuing government and foundation programs with measurable environmental outcomes.

Funding eligibility and availability depend on the individual program, funder priorities, and your organization's profile. We assess every opportunity with you before you commit time.
