Our Services

End-to-end grant support under one roof

Eight service lines that carry your organization from opportunities identification to outcome impact assessment. Engage us for the complete lifecycle or the stage where you need us most.

Book a Consultation

Grant Research and Opportunity Identification

Foundation of every win

We continuously scan federal, state, foundation, and corporate funding landscapes to surface opportunities that genuinely fit your organization, not just opportunities that exist.

  • Federal, state, foundation, and corporate sources
  • Deadline and cycle tracking
  • Fit scoring against your mission and capacity
  • Ongoing opportunity pipeline

Grant Strategy and Eligibility Assessment

Before any application, we map a realistic strategy: which programs match your structure and location, what each funder expects, and where your probability of success is strongest.

  • Eligibility screening for every opportunity
  • Funding roadmap and calendar
  • Go or no-go recommendations
  • Positioning against funder priorities

Professional Grant Writing and Application Preparation

Compelling, funder-aligned narratives, budgets, logic models, and attachments, developed to the exact requirements of each application and polished for reviewers.

  • Narratives and needs statements
  • Budgets and justifications
  • Logic models and evaluation plans
  • Supporting documents and appendices

Grant Application Management and Submission

Portal registrations, system-for-award requirements, internal sign-offs, and deadline logistics, all coordinated so your application lands complete and on time.

  • Portal setup and registration guidance
  • Checklist and document assembly
  • Internal review coordination
  • Deadline and submission tracking

Grant Approval and Award Management

Most consultancies disappear after submission. We stay with you through reviewer questions, award negotiation, agreements, and first-dollar requirements.

  • Reviewer question response support
  • Award terms and agreement review support
  • Onboarding to award requirements
  • Set-up of tracking and documentation

Grant Administration and Compliance Support

Ongoing administration that keeps your award in good standing: documentation, funder communications, spending alignment, and condition tracking.

  • Grant file and records organization
  • Funder correspondence support
  • Condition and milestone tracking
  • Amendment and modification support

Progress Reporting, Final Reporting, and Grant Closeout

Reporting is where future funding is won or lost. We help you tell a clear impact story, meet every deadline, and close out grants cleanly.

  • Progress and interim report drafting
  • Final reports and impact narratives
  • Closeout documentation support
  • Lessons learned for the next cycle

Specialized Healthcare, Startup, Fellowship, and Innovation Funding

A dedicated practice area for healthcare organizations, early-stage companies, and innovators pursuing fellowships, SBIR-style programs, and innovation-focused funders.

  • Healthcare and medical technology programs
  • Startup and accelerator funding
  • Fellowship applications
  • Innovation and research-focused programs

One partner. Every stage.

Whether you need a single application or a year-round funding function, Agiele Funding scales with you.
