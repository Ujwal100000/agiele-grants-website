FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how we work. If your question is not here, book a consultation and ask us directly.

Do you only write grant applications?

No. Grant writing is one of eight service lines. We cover the complete lifecycle: research and opportunity identification, strategy and eligibility, writing and application preparation, submission management, award management, administration and compliance, and progress reporting through final reports and closeout.

How do I know if my organization is eligible for grants?

Eligibility depends on each program's rules: organization type, location, size, revenue, project focus, and more. As part of our strategy and eligibility assessment, we screen every opportunity against your profile before you invest time in an application.

Can startups really win grants?

Yes, many funding programs are designed specifically for early-stage companies, including fellowships, pitch-linked programs, and innovation-focused funding. Grants are highly competitive and approval is never guaranteed, but a disciplined, well-prepared application materially improves your position.

Do you work with healthcare organizations?

Healthcare and medical technology is one of our featured practice areas. We support healthcare providers, health tech companies, and life sciences organizations pursuing program funding, innovation grants, and fellowships, with applications framed around outcomes, compliance, and patient impact.

What kinds of funding are available to growing businesses?

Growing businesses can pursue federal and state program grants, research and innovation funding, export and workforce development programs, and industry-specific initiatives. The right mix depends on your industry, revenue stage, and capacity, which is exactly what our research and strategy stage establishes.

What is the difference between federal and state funding?

Federal funding tends to be larger and more competitive, with detailed compliance requirements. State funding is often more accessible for local organizations and frequently flows through state agencies. Most successful funding strategies combine federal, state, foundation, and corporate sources.

What does application preparation involve?

Depending on the program: narratives and needs statements, budgets and justifications, logic models, evaluation plans, letters of support, and supporting attachments, all assembled to the funder's exact format and submitted through the required portal.

Do you help after an application is submitted?

Yes. We support clients through the approval stage: responding to reviewer questions, understanding award terms, and completing first-dollar requirements. After an award, we continue with administration, compliance, progress reporting, final reports, and closeout.

What happens during final reporting and closeout?

Closeout confirms to the funder that the work was completed and funds were used appropriately: final financial reports, outcome summaries, and required documentation. A clean closeout protects your reputation and your eligibility for future funding.

How long does the grant process take?

It varies widely. Foundation applications may move in weeks; federal programs can take several months from deadline to award decision. Timelines are set by each funder, and we map them clearly in your funding strategy so you know what to expect.

How much does your support cost?

We provide customized support with transparent engagements. Pricing depends on project scope, application complexity, and the level of support required, and it is agreed clearly before work begins. Book a consultation for a quote tailored to your needs.

Do you guarantee grant approval?

No. Grant decisions rest entirely with funders, and no consultant can guarantee approval. What we can guarantee is disciplined research, honest eligibility feedback, professionally prepared applications, and dependable support through every stage that follows.

Do you work with organizations outside your home state?

Yes. We support organizations across all 50 U.S. states, working remotely and on your schedule. Use our interactive state map or directory to find your state and start the conversation.
